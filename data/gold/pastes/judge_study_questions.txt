Question
"Amy casts Assassin's Trophy targeting Nihil Spellbomb. Nico searches for a Snow-Covered Swamp and wants to tap it to pay for the triggered ability of Nihil Spellbomb. Can they do this?"
Answer
"Yes. Assassin's Trophy will resolve completely before Nihil Spellbomb's trigger is placed onto the stack (603.3,117.2a). The payment of {b} is made as the trigger resolves. (603.5)"

Question:
"Aubree has Murderous Rider in their graveyard. Can they flash back Murderous Rider's Adventure with Flashback?"
Answer:
"No. An Adventure defines alternative characteristics the card can have when cast as an Adventure. (715.2) Adventurer cards only have their normal characteristics in every zone other than the stack (715.4), so it's not a legal target for Flashback."
Cards Mentioned:
[Murderous Rider, Flashback]


Question:
"Nylah controls Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth. Alex controls no lands and plays Castle Locthwain. Does it enter tapped or untapped?"
Answer:
"Tapped. The game determines how replacement effects apply before the event in question occurs (614.4), and at that time, Alex controls no Swamps.

(614.12 means that the game considers the Castle Locthwain to be a Swamp itself for these purposes, but it's not on the battlefield yet, so this doesn't matter.)"
Cards Mentioned:
['Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth', Castle Locthwain]


Question:
"Armando controls Leyline of the Guildpact and casts Magus of the Moon. What are the characteristics of Snow-Covered Plains, Gloomlake Verge, Dryad Arbor, and Wastes after Magus of the Moon resolves?"
Answer:
"Snow-Covered Plains and Wastes have all basic land subtypes: "Plains", "Island", "Swamp", "Mountain", and "Forest". (305.6) Leyline of the Guildpact doesn't change supertypes and Magus of the Moon affects nonbasic lands. (305.7)

Gloomlake Verge has subtype Mountain. (305.7) There are no dependencies between Leyline of the Guildpact and Magus of the Moon, so we apply the type-changing effects in timestamp order. (613.1d, 613.8a)

Dryad Arbor is now a Creature Land - Dryad Mountain. Gaining a basic land type doesn't remove other card types or nonland subtypes. (305.7)"
Cards Mentioned:
[Leyline of the Guildpact, Magus of the Moon, Snow-Covered Plains, Gloomlake Verge, Dryad Arbor, Wastes]

Question:
"Can Nelson cast Pact of Negation targeting Avery's Ox of Agonas that is escaping from their graveyard?"
Answer:
"Yes. Escape is an alternative cost (702.138a), which means that once paid the escaped spell is on the stack, and can be interacted with as normal. Countering a spell means to remove that spell from the stack before it resolves. (701.6a) Ox of Agonas is a legal target for Pact of Negation."
Cards Mentioned:
[Pact of Negation, Ox of Agonas]

Question:
"In a game of Two-Headed Giant, both Adalyn and Aspen have no cards left in their library. Adalyn controls Jace, Wielder of Mysteries. What happens in their draw step?"
Answer:
"Their team wins the game. Both players would draw their card at the same time. (805.4b) Jace, Wielder of Mysteries replaces Adalyn's draw with winning the game. Since their team has won (810.8a), Aspen doesn't lose, since that doesn't happen until state-based actions are checked. (704.5b/121.4)"

['Jace, Wielder of Mysteries']

Question:
"Nico controls Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite. Adelina casts Murderous Cut targeting Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite. In response, Nico casts Ephemerate targeting Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite. After everything has resolved, is Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite on the battlefield?"

Question:
"Alice controls Ral, Monsoon Mage and casts Past in Flames for its Flashback cost. Will Ral, Monsoon Mage decrease the cost they have to pay?"
Answer:
"Yes. Flashback is an alternative cost to cast a spell. (702.34a) When casting Past in Flames, Alice will first determine the base cost, which in this case is the flashback cost. After that, they will apply all cost increases and reductions. (601.2f)"
Cards Mentioned:
['Ral, Monsoon Mage', Past in Flames]

Question:

[Karn Liberated, Hydroelectric Specimen, Hydroelectric Laboratory]
